# Sending from hello@monochromeworlds.com

Mail sent from a Gmail address through MailerLite lands in spam, and no amount
of tweaking the email itself will fix it. Gmail publishes a policy saying only
Google may send as `gmail.com`; when MailerLite sends "from" a Gmail address,
that check (DMARC) fails and inboxes treat the message as forged.

Sending from your own domain fixes it, because you can prove MailerLite is
allowed to send on your behalf.

There are two separate jobs, and they're easy to confuse:

- **Sending as `hello@`** — DNS records that authenticate MailerLite. This is
  what stops the spam foldering.
- **Receiving at `hello@`** — a mailbox or forwarding, so replies reach you.
  Set this up too: a from-address that bounces looks bad and hurts your sender
  reputation.

DNS for `monochromeworlds.com` lives at Porkbun, so every record below is added
there (Porkbun → your domain → **DNS**). These records are independent of the
`A`/`CNAME` records pointing the site at Vercel — you're adding, not replacing.

---

## 1. Receive mail at hello@ (Porkbun, free)

Porkbun includes free email forwarding.

1. Porkbun → your domain → **Email Forwarding**
2. Forward `hello@monochromeworlds.com` → your personal Gmail
3. Save, then send a test message to `hello@monochromeworlds.com` and confirm it
   arrives

This adds `MX` records. They don't affect the website.

> Want to *send* replies as hello@ from inside Gmail? Gmail → Settings → Accounts
> → "Send mail as". You'll need SMTP credentials; Porkbun forwarding alone is
> receive-only.

## 2. Authenticate the domain in MailerLite

1. MailerLite → **Settings** → **Domains** (or "Sending domains")
2. Add `monochromeworlds.com` and start **domain authentication**
3. MailerLite shows you SPF and DKIM records **specific to your account** —
   keep this page open, you'll copy from it

Never copy these values from a guide (including this one): the DKIM key is
unique per account.

## 3. Add MailerLite's records at Porkbun

Porkbun → your domain → **DNS** → add each record MailerLite listed.

- **Type**: match exactly what MailerLite says (`TXT` or `CNAME`)
- **Host**: Porkbun wants the part *before* your domain. If MailerLite says
  `ml._domainkey.monochromeworlds.com`, enter `ml._domainkey`. For the root,
  leave Host empty.
- **Answer/Value**: paste MailerLite's value exactly — no added quotes or spaces

Then go back to MailerLite and click **Verify**. DNS can take anywhere from a
few minutes to a few hours; if verification fails, wait and retry before
changing anything.

> Already have an SPF record (a `TXT` starting `v=spf1`)? Don't add a second
> one — a domain may only have one. Merge MailerLite's include into the
> existing record instead.

## 4. Add a DMARC record

SPF and DKIM prove the mail is yours; DMARC tells inboxes what to do about mail
that isn't. Having one improves placement, and Gmail and Yahoo now expect it.

Add a `TXT` record at Porkbun:

- **Host**: `_dmarc`
- **Value**: `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:hello@monochromeworlds.com`

`p=none` monitors without blocking anything — the right place to start. Once
you've confirmed your mail passes for a few weeks, you can tighten it to
`p=quarantine`.

## 5. Switch the sender

Only after MailerLite shows the domain as verified:

1. Automations → your automation → **Email 1**
2. **Sender email**: `hello@monochromeworlds.com`
3. **Who is it from**: `Monochrome Worlds`
4. Save. Repeat anywhere else a sender is set (account defaults, other emails).

## 6. Verify it actually worked

- Send yourself a test, open it in Gmail, then ⋮ → **Show original**. You want
  **PASS** on all three: SPF, DKIM, DMARC.
- Or send a test to [mail-tester.com](https://www.mail-tester.com) for a score
  out of 10 and a list of anything still weak.

## Keeping mail out of spam after that

- **Don't switch to `no-reply@`.** Replies are a positive signal; `hello@` is
  the better choice.
- **Let people actually confirm.** Double opt-in produces a cleaner list and
  better long-term placement.
- **Keep the list clean.** Never import bought or scraped addresses — one spam
  trap can undo all of this.
- **Send consistently.** Long silences followed by a burst look like spam.
- **The template already helps**: real text (not one big image), a plain-text
  version generated by MailerLite, a working unsubscribe link, and a linked PDF
  rather than an attachment.
